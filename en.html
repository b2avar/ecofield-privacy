ecoField Privacy Policy and Data Protection Notice

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

This notice explains, in accordance with Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), which personal data is processed when you use the ecoField app, for what purposes and on which legal grounds. The rules for using the app are set out separately in the Terms of Use (EULA).

1. Data Controller

Your personal data is processed by Burak Avar, the developer of the ecoField app, as the data controller. Contact: burakavar@gmail.com

2. Personal Data We Process

  • Account information: name, email address, an irreversible hash of your password, your Google or Apple account identifier if you sign in with them, profile photo, language preference.
  • Scan and discovery data: photos you take with your camera or choose from your gallery, the location (GPS) where the photo was taken, AI analysis results, time travel images.
  • Game and community data: points, level, badges, quests, home camp location, friends list, community posts, likes, report and block records.
  • Usage and technical data: in-app usage events (for example which screen was opened, whether a scan was started and completed), device and app version information, IP address and server request logs.

3. Purposes and Legal Grounds

  • Creating your account, letting you sign in, analysing scans, saving your discoveries, providing the map, quests and community features: establishment and performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Measuring and improving how the app is used, finding errors, keeping the service secure and preventing abuse: legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).
  • Reviewing reported content and removing it where necessary, responding to legal requests: legal obligation and legitimate interest (KVKK Art. 5/2-ç, 5/2-f).
  • Sending service emails such as password resets: performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). We do not send you advertising or promotional emails.

Camera, photo and location access is used only when you allow it on your device; you can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings.

4. Recipients and Transfers Abroad

To provide the service, your data is shared with the following service providers, only as far as needed for the relevant purpose:

  • Google Cloud (Gemini AI service via Vertex AI): scan photos are sent for analysis without your name, email address or account ID. Because the photo content is not altered, we recommend making sure that people or private spaces are not visible in your scans.
  • Cloudinary: storage of photos and profile images.
  • Render and MongoDB: hosting of the app server and database.
  • PostHog: usage analytics (usage events, device information and a random user ID; your name and email address are not sent).
  • SenderTR (servers in Turkey): sending service emails.
  • Google and Apple: authentication, if you choose to sign in with these accounts.
  • Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap: only location coordinates, without any identity information, to obtain weather and place names.

Because the servers of these providers are located outside Turkey (mainly in the United States and the European Union), your data is transferred abroad. We are working to put in place the standard contracts provided for in Article 9 of the KVKK with these providers. Your data is disclosed to competent public authorities only where required by law; your data is never sold.

5. Community Content and Reports

Photos and texts you share in the community can be seen by other users. You can report a post you believe is inappropriate or infringes your copyright with the in-app report option or by writing to burakavar@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed; posts reported by several users are hidden until the review is completed, and content found to be unlawful is removed.

6. Retention Periods

  • Your account data is kept for as long as your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, your account, discoveries, time travels, posts and their photos are deleted. When you delete a single discovery, its photo is deleted too.
  • Data created when you use the app without an account (as a guest), including the guest account, scans, photos and location information, is deleted automatically if you do not create an account within thirty days of your last activity.
  • Usage analytics data and server logs are kept for the retention period of the relevant service provider and deleted afterwards.

7. Your Rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred, to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, to request its deletion or destruction, to request that these actions be notified to the third parties it was transferred to, to object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights, write to burakavar@gmail.com. Your request will be answered free of charge within thirty days at the latest. If your request is rejected or you find the answer insufficient, you may file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board. You can update your profile information in the app and delete your account from the Profile screen.

8. Data Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored in an irreversible form and access to data is limited to authorised persons. Please note, however, that no method of transmission over the Internet or of storage is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The current version is always published on this page and the date above is updated.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this notice: burakavar@gmail.com
